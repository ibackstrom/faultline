02 / HOW IT WORKS

Bring one failure.
Keep the test.

Start with a case that's hard to replay. We work with your existing tests and tools.

  1. 01

    Reproduce it.

    Agree on what should happen. Replay the failure locally or in staging, using synthetic data.

  2. 02

    Investigate the cause.

    Inspect the failing path. Simplify the case where possible and propose a correction when the evidence supports it.

  3. 03

    Check the fix.

    Rerun the same case and controls. Hand over the test, commands and before/after results.

Discuss a pilot ↗

A few useful boundaries.

Is this a software platform?

No. It's an engineering service. We work with your tools and hand over tests and results. The examples above are local component investigations, not full customer-agent evaluations.

Do we need to replace our eval tools?

No. If Promptfoo, LangSmith, Braintrust or your own tests already solve the problem, there may be nothing for us to add. The useful job is the reproduction and fix check your current process still leaves to an engineer.

We already run Langfuse, Promptfoo or Opik. What's left for you?

Those platforms record traces, score answers and gate pass rates. Their public docs today show no path from one failing trace to a minimal case, or to a fix check with paired controls. That narrow engineering job is what we deliver, and the test lands in your existing runner.

What isn't included by default?

Production writes, live third-party security testing, model training and access to private customer records. This isn't a security certification or a promise that the rest of the system is safe. New access, provider spend and testing scope need agreement first.
